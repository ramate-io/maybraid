# Training Ground

Selecting Training Ground in the Maybraid executable seats the saved character
on a pinned FinePatch of the Durham / Chico / Richmond stack (four 160 m origin
cells, seed 42). One seeded single-terrace Richmond development sits at the
origin inside a flat courtyard that is composed onto the FinePatch mesh. A
level 20 m wall of POM ashlar (`stone` urban recipe) encloses the courtyard.

The player spawns on the courtyard band between the building and the wall. Two
six-member Brawler mobs flank the player along the band, one on each side,
with the nearest member 10 m away and 3 m between members. Brawler mobs belong
to the FFA group and are hostile to it, so they fight the player, the other mob,
and their own packmates from the first frame. World mob streaming is off while
Training is live. Hopscotch also stays off, and a Training pose is not written.

Leave restores the playable-world fill and returns home.
