# 3.4.7: Well-known Upper Canopy Groves

This page is subsection **3.4.7** of [RFC-183: Chico Vegetation](../../README.md)

Well-known upper-canopy groves define the dominant tree layer: skyline forests, cultivated tree rows, sparse savanna trees, palms, riparian corridors, and giant canopy systems that can sit above lower-canopy massives.

> [!NOTE]
> Assume an empty grove variant exists. 

Subsections:

- [3.4.7.1: Jungle Massives](./01-jungle-massives/README.md): moderate-density giant jungle upper canopy above Jungle Lower Massives.
- [3.4.7.2: Conifer Massives](./02-conifer-massives/README.md): low-density giant evergreen upper canopy above Conifer Lower Massives.
- [3.4.7.3: Temperate Massives](./03-temperate-massives/README.md): low-density giant broadleaf upper canopy above Temperate Lower Massives.
- [3.4.7.4: Riparian General](./04-riparian-general/README.md): moderate-density mixed river-corridor canopy with Braid Oak, Storybook Tree, and rare High Bush forms.
- [3.4.7.5: Rolling Oaks](./05-rolling-oaks/README.md): low-density open oak canopy with common Braid Oak and rare Storybook Tree variants.
- [3.4.7.6: Forlorn Savanna](./06-forlorn-savanna/README.md): low-density dry savanna canopy with Rory, acacia-like High Bush, and rare Storybook Tree variants.
- [3.4.7.7: Orchard](./07-orchard/README.md): moderate-density cultivated Storybook Tree grove with fruiting textures and low cell offset.
- [3.4.7.8: Vineyard](./08-vineyard/README.md): moderate-density low Rory-trained vineyard grove with grape-like fruiting textures and low cell offset.
- [3.4.7.9: Date Grove](./09-date-grove/README.md): moderate-density cultivated Date Palm grove with fruiting textures and low cell offset.
- [3.4.7.10: Palm Shade](./10-palm-shade/README.md): low-density palm canopy with Waialea Palm and Date Palm variants.
- [3.4.7.11: Riparian Mix](./11-riparian-mix/README.md): mixed riparian canopy adding common conifer variants to the general river-corridor set.
- [3.4.7.12: Alpine](./12-alpine/README.md): moderate-density cold upland conifer canopy with common Friend's Conifer and less common Liam's Conifer.
- [3.4.7.13: Dryland](./13-dryland/README.md): very-low-density arid canopy with Liam's Conifer and Vase Tree variants.
- [3.4.7.14: Storyteller's](./14-storyteller-s/README.md): moderate-density colorful Storybook Tree and Braid Oak canopy.
- [3.4.7.15: Trade Winds](./15-trade-winds/README.md): low-density tropical canopy with Storybook Tree, banyans, and rare Waialea Palm.
- [3.4.7.16: Wandering Acacia](./16-wandering-acacia/README.md): very-low-density dry open canopy with acacia-like High Bush and dry Sope's Banyan variants.
- [3.4.7.17: Leeward](./17-leeward/README.md): moderate-density sheltered canopy with Temperate Conifer and Storybook Tree variants.
- [3.4.7.18: Christmas Taiga](./18-christmas-taiga/README.md): moderate-density cold Northern Conifer canopy.
