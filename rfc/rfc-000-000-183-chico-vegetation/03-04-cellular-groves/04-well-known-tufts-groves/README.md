# 3.4.4: Well-known Tufts Groves

This page is subsection **3.4.4** of [RFC-183: Chico Vegetation](../../README.md)

Well-known tufts groves are ready-made **tuft-layer** recipes: each combines the shared [Tuft](../../03-01-stalk-and-ball-stick-trees/02-ball-components/06-tufts/README.md) construction with density, height, palette, and optional companion constructions for recognizable grass and scrub identities.

> [!NOTE]
> Assume `None` is available in each grove distribution when empty cells are needed.

Subsections:

- [3.4.4.1: Common Tufts](./01-common-tufts/README.md): sparse, low-to-moderate clumps for overlays on ground cover or primary grass in arid regions; a few material/shape varietals.
- [3.4.4.2: Tall Grass](./02-tall-grass/README.md): dense ~50–100 cm tufts for tropical grasslands, river edges, wet meadows, and lush transitions.
- [3.4.4.3: Bush Scrub](./03-bush-scrub/README.md): tufts plus small [Common High Bush](../../03-01-stalk-and-ball-stick-trees/07-well-known-tree-constructions/12-common-high-bush/README.md); tall varietals, sparse placement.
- [3.4.4.4: Wild Grass](./04-wild-grass/README.md): dense tall stalks with many colorful varietals; valleys, prairies, tropics, and open fields.
- [3.4.4.5: Tropical Tufts](./05-tropical-tufts/README.md): shorter lush tufts with small [Palm Bush](../../03-01-stalk-and-ball-stick-trees/07-well-known-tree-constructions/10-palm-bush/README.md); sparse placement, richness from surrounds.
