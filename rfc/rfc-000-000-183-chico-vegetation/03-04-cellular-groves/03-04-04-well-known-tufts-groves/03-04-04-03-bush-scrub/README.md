# 3.4.4.3: Bush Scrub

- Use [Tuft](../../../03-01-stalk-and-ball-stick-trees/03-01-02-ball-components/03-01-02-06-tufts/README.md) and small [Common High Bush](../../../03-01-stalk-and-ball-stick-trees/03-01-07-well-known-tree-constructions/03-01-07-12-common-high-bush/README.md) constructions.
