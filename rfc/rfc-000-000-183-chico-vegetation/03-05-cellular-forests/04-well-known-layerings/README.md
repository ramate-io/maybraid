# 3.5.4: Well-known Layerings

This page is subsection **3.5.4** of [RFC-183: Chico Vegetation](../../README.md)

Well-known layerings are reusable forest-cell recipes. Each layering defines compatible distributions for the ground cover, tufts, understory, lower canopy, and upper canopy layers. These are selected by forest-level [Hopscotch](../02-selection/README.md#3521-hopscotch), then each layer selects its grove with [Bucket Throw](../../03-04-cellular-groves/03-04-02-selection-and-placement/03-04-02-01-bucket-throw/README.md).

Subsections:

* [3.5.4.1: Lush Jungle](./01-lush-jungle/README.md): dense tropical layering from the original forest-layer example.
* [3.5.4.2: Riparian](./02-riparian/README.md): basic river-corridor layering with wet ground cover, green understory, and riparian canopy.
* [3.5.4.3: Taiga](./03-taiga/README.md): cold conifer layering mixing alpine, scrubland, and Christmas Taiga with minimal understory.
* [3.5.4.4: Liam's Summer](./04-liam-s-summer/README.md): tropical summer layering over Monster Grass and Braid Grass with occasional palms and dry-canopy surprises.
* [3.5.4.5: Owl's Desert](./05-owl-s-desert/README.md): sparse desert layering with high `None` odds and rare oasis, scrub, and acacia pockets.
* [3.5.4.6: Mi Robles](./06-mi-robles/README.md): open oak layering with common Rolling Oaks, Allbed, and rare understory accents.
* [3.5.4.7: Seceda](./07-seceda/README.md): alpine scrub-conifer layering with common chaparral, High Bush, arid saplings, Alpine, and Allbed.
* [3.5.4.8: Kumulipo](./08-kumulipo/README.md): sacred tropical layering with common Shamanhome and Palm Shade, sparse understory, and rare canopy surprises.
* [3.5.4.9: Waiguo](./09-waiguo/README.md): cultivated mixed grove layering with Orchard, Vineyard, Date Grove, and Braid Grass.
* [3.5.4.10: Ag Town](./10-ag-town/README.md): cultivated settlement-edge layering with common orchard, vineyard, and date rows over mostly empty lower layers.
* [3.5.4.11: Sun's Barren](./11-sun-s-barren/README.md): almost-empty barren layering with rare Grassy Mounds.
* [3.5.4.12: Temperate Holy](./12-temperate-holy/README.md): sparse sacred giant-tree layering with common Temperate Lower Massives.
* [3.5.4.13: Old Steppe](./13-old-steppe/README.md): open grassland layering with common Allbed and Grassy Mounds, plus rare Conifer Sapling.
* [3.5.4.14: Trap Thicket](./14-trap-thicket/README.md): dense tropical thicket layering with Unending Jungle and heavy understory.
* [3.5.4.15: Bush](./15-bush/README.md): dry brush-and-open-tree layering with common Low Bush, High Bush, Forlorn Savanna, and Wandering Acacia.
* [3.5.4.16: Old Nevada](./16-old-nevada/README.md): sparse dry conifer layering with common Arid Conifer Sapling and Grassy Mounds.
* [3.5.4.17: Storybook](./17-storybook/README.md): whimsical mixed layering with common Riparian Mix and less common colorful canopy options.
* [3.5.4.18: Meadowland](./18-meadowland/README.md): open meadow layering with common Huelgoat Pitch and rare canopy events.
