# Drumstick

![Drumstick](./assets/drumstick.jpeg)

The drumstick lower limb mesh or multi-mesh is a simple design found in nature. It is roughly a cylinder, but with a bulbus region.

## Parameters

- `height`: the total height of the drumstick.
- `depth`: the depth of the drumstick.
- `width`: the width of the drumstick.
- `drop`: the distance from the top of the drumstick to the bottom of the bulbus region.

Detail can be added to give geodesic or noisy lumps. 

You could also offset the top of the bulbus regions `offset`.
