# Chain of pearls — Crozon mouth

**Canonical primitive:** [Chain of pearls (common)](../../common/chain-of-pearls/README.md)

## Mouth embedding

**Teeth ridge**, **serration**, or **radula** abstraction: pearls along the **jaw line** (upper or lower); overlap for **continuous saw**, gap for **pearly** teeth. Typically, **child** meshes parent to **jaw** bones with count driven by species seed.
