# Spheroid — Crozon torso

**Canonical primitive:** [Spheroid (common)](../../common/spheroid/README.md)

## Torso embedding

Use for a **simple belly**, **stylized trunk mass**, or **secondary bulk** (e.g. layered with a tapered-cuboid chest). Center often sits **below** the chest bulge and **above** the leg split; squash vertically (`ry` smaller than `rx`/`rz`) for a **pancake** torso or invert for an upright **egg**.

Parent the ellipsoid **center** to a **spine** or **pelvis** bone per rig; avoid intersecting the [Tapered cuboid](../tapered-cuboid/README.md) or [Drumstick](../drumstick/README.md) hull unless the species intentionally overlaps volumes for soft-body read.
