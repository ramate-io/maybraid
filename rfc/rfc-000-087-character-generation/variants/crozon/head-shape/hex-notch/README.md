# Hex notch — Crozon head shape

**Canonical primitive:** [Hex notch (common)](../../common/hex-notch/README.md)

## Head embedding

Use this when you want a **mildly exotic** head read without going fully alien: the broad **hex** crown keeps the form grounded, while the lower **triangular cutout** suggests soft **tendril** roots, barbels, or hanging facial appendages. Keep the notch centered on the lower face plane for a clean read, then attach [mouth](../../mouth/README.md) / [nose-snout](../../nose-snout/README.md) volumes above or around the notch depending on species.
