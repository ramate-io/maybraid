# Rounded cone — Crozon head shape

**Canonical primitive:** [Rounded cone (common)](../../common/rounded-cone/README.md)

## Head embedding

Use as an **angled-downward** head mass when you want a directional snout/brow read without a horn-like point. Keep the broad end near the cranium and tilt the taper axis nose-down, so the rounded cap terminates toward muzzle/chin space with a softer, creature-friendly silhouette.

This sits between [cylinder](../cylinder/README.md) and [egg](../egg/README.md): more directional than egg, less harsh than a sharp cone.
