# Dish — Crozon head shape

**Canonical primitive:** [Dish (common)](../../common/dish/README.md)

## Head embedding

Use the **inverted** orientation from [ear](../../ear/dish/README.md): **flip** the frame, so the **opening** faces **up**, and the **cavity** hangs **down** over the upper face. The **rim** seals a **brow**, **hairline**, or **helmet** slice; shallow `depth` reads as a **shallow vault**, deeper `depth` as a **bucket** cranium (cartoon or armored).

Blend the **rim** into [cylinder](../cylinder/README.md) or [tapered-cuboid](../tapered-cuboid/README.md) cheek masses; keep [nose-snout](../../nose-snout/README.md) and [mouth](../../mouth/README.md) **below** the rim plane, so features do not intersect the cap interior.
