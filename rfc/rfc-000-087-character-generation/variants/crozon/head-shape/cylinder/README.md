# Cylinder — Crozon head shape

**Canonical primitive:** [Cylinder (common)](../../common/cylinder/README.md)

## Head embedding

**Elongated / tubular** cranial reads: **horse**, **fish**, or stylized **helmet** silhouettes. Axis usually along **face forward** or **snout**; cap **top** at brow attachment, **bottom** toward [nose-snout](../../nose-snout/README.md) or mouth seam. **Lateral wall bow** can suggest **cranial bulge** midsegment without switching to [Bow](../../common/bow/README.md) (bent spine).
