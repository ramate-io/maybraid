# Egg — Crozon head shape

**Canonical primitive:** [Egg (common)](../../common/egg/README.md)

## Head embedding

**Egg or teardrop cranium:** orient **`long_axis`** roughly **spine-up** or **face-forward** per species; **wide** pole at the **back** or **top** of the skull, **narrow** pole toward **muzzle** or **chin** for a soft taper into [nose-snout](../../nose-snout/README.md). Reads more **organic** than [spheroid](../spheroid/README.md) alone when you want a clear **jaw–cranium** gradient without [tapered-cuboid](../tapered-cuboid/README.md) corners.
