# Curling cone — Crozon ear

**Canonical primitive:** [Curling cone (common)](../../common/curling-cone/README.md)

## Ear embedding

**Tightly rolled** helix tips (some **bat**, **elf**, stylized **lamb** ears): small `base_radius` and **short** `spine_length` so the curl reads at head scale without dominating the silhouette. Root the cone at the **helix** or **antihelix** ridge on the [dish](../dish/README.md) or [cylinder](../cylinder/README.md) scaffold, with the curl plane roughly **tangential** to the head.

Contrast with [chain-of-pearls](../chain-of-pearls/README.md) (beaded ear) and [bow](../../common/bow/README.md) on the ear (broad curved sheet): curling cone emphasizes a **tapered tube** that **hooks** in space.
