# Rounded cone — Crozon ear

**Canonical primitive:** [Rounded cone (common)](../../common/rounded-cone/README.md)

## Ear embedding

Good for ears that taper from a thicker root to a soft tip, such as folded cartilage fins or mammal-like cones without a sharp point. Mount the broad end at the head socket and aim the rounded end up/back or lateral depending on alertness and species profile.

Use `axis_bend` for gentle ear curl, and keep `cap_roundness` moderate to preserve a clear ear tip silhouette.
