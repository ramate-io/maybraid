# Chain of pearls — Crozon ear

**Canonical primitive:** [Chain of pearls (common)](../../common/chain-of-pearls/README.md)

## Ear embedding

**Segmented** or **larval** ears: pearls along a short arc from **head socket** to **tip**; overlap for a fused **caterpillar** ear or space slightly for **beaded** jewelry-like silhouettes. Rig as a short **chain** of bones or one bendy handle parented to the head.
